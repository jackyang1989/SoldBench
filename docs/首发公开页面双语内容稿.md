## Methodology ｜ 定价方法

**EN** Search for a product and choose the market, time window, condition and price basis. We show how many distinct sale observations were assessed, how many qualify as comparable, and how many have a verified price for your chosen basis. We keep an accepted Best Offer amount unknown when the source cannot establish it. If your price includes shipping and the required shipping amount is unknown, the sale does not contribute to that price calculation.

**简体中文** 输入商品，选择市场、时间窗口、成色与价格口径。结果显示评估了多少条去重后的成交样本、其中多少可比、以及多少条有符合所选口径的核验价格。来源无法证实 Best Offer 的实际成交价时，金额标为未知。如果你的价格口径包含运费，所需运费未知的成交不参与该口径的价格计算。

**EN · Synthetic example** At 2026-11-14T06:00:00Z, 64 of 121 assessed samples were comparable. The 58 verified prices give a median of USD 58.00. Two separate illustrations show how missing evidence affects the calculation. A displayed ask of USD 20.00 with an unknown accepted offer does not enter the median. Under an item-plus-shipping basis, a USD 40.00 sale with unknown shipping also stays outside that median. We show no central estimate with fewer than 8 verified prices. Results with 8–29 are limited samples. A result with at least 30 has sufficient sample size under this rule. These counts describe the sample available to SoldBench. Market-wide sales and coverage may be unknown.

**简体中文 · 合成示例** 截至 2026-11-14T06:00:00Z，121 条已评估样本中有 64 条可比。58 条核验价格的中位数是 USD 58.00。以下两笔独立示意说明缺少证据时如何计算。挂牌价 USD 20.00 但实际议价成交价未知，挂牌价不进入中位数。在商品价加运费口径下，商品成交价 USD 40.00、运费未知的记录也不进入该中位数。核验价格少于 8 条时不显示中心估计，8–29 条为有限样本，至少 30 条达到本规则的足量门槛。这些数量描述 SoldBench 能取得的样本，全市场销量和覆盖范围可能未知。

**确认与签收** 统计计算遵循完整快照；页面若另给平均值或 5% 截尾均值，分别标注。121/64/58 是黄金夹具数量，USD 20.00 和 USD 40.00 是排除逻辑示意。发布字段为获准来源、窗口、实际覆盖、版本及更新时间，数据负责人和来源负责人核对。两语金额、分母与结果从同一夹具生成。[统计契约](搜索页采集字段与统计契约.md)

## Data Availability ｜ 数据可用性

**EN** Choose a market and category to see its permitted query window and the time of the most recent usable data. Some fields or categories may be unavailable. If collection is delayed, an eligible older result may be available after you confirm, free of charge. Your research history may include earlier results with their original dates. If a source withdraws permission, affected results and images can become unavailable even in history or through an existing share link.

**简体中文** 选择市场和类目，查看获准查询的时间窗口与最近可用数据时点。部分字段或类目可能不可用。采集延迟时，如有符合条件的陈旧结果，可在你确认后免费查看。研究历史可能包含带原时点的较早结果。来源撤回许可后，受影响的结果和图片可能在历史及现有分享链接中变为不可用。

## 开发者与 Agent 入口

**EN** Create an API key in your account and keep it in a local environment variable or your secret store. Submit the synthetic query from Quickstart and keep its request and job IDs. A 202 response means the search is still processing. Poll the original job, then explicitly confirm and claim the result when required. Reopen the delivered result for free and check the actual charge in Usage. On 429, follow Retry-After. On 503, retry within the client budget and use the original identifiers if a response is uncertain. Revoke lost keys in your account.

**简体中文** 在账户内创建 API 密钥，并将其保存在本地环境变量或密钥库。提交快速开始中的合成查询，保留请求及作业编号。202 表示查询仍在处理，轮询原作业，必要时明确同意费用后领取结果。可免费复取已交付结果，并在用量页核对实际扣费。遇到 429 按 Retry-After 等待，遇到 503 在客户端预算内重试，响应不明时沿用原编号。密钥遗失请在账户内撤销。

**确认与签收** 示例默认本机合成环境，REST、Web 与 MCP 费用共用同一规则。开发者负责人核对三客户端、两宿主、失败恢复、密钥范围与实际响应，发布页仅替换已验收的域名和支持版本。[客户端与宿主验收](MCP与开发者交付契约.md)
