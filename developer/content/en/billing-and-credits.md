# Credits and payment boundaries

At most 1 credit per effective standard query, never per row. Same-owner canonical query reuse lasts 10 minutes. Queueing, failures, insufficient samples, stale, compute polling and repeated delivery are free. Expiring promo/subscription buckets spend earliest expiry first; same-expiry subscription first, purchased last. Ledger entries are immutable. Candidate commercial plans are not an approved price list.

Stripe is primary; PayPal supplemental. Creem/Dodo are qualification reserves, never automatic payment failover. This build runs offline payment simulations only: no real charge, payout, product approval or activation. UI language never changes credits, country, currency or query identity.
